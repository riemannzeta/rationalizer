Author & Publication Rankings

Authors and publications ranked by their balance score—a measure of how consistent their emotional valence is across different topic areas. Higher scores indicate more balanced coverage; lower scores suggest more variation in tone between topics.

Balance Score Calculation

Balance score is calculated as: 1 / (1 + cross_category_variance × 10)

Authors with low variance across categories (similar emotional tone regardless of topic) score near 1.0. Authors whose tone varies significantly between topics score closer to 0.
