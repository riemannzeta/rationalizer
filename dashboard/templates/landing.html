The Conjugate Principle

In complex number theory, multiplying a complex number by its conjugate yields a real number. For example, (3 + 4i) × (3 - 4i) = 25. The imaginary components cancel out, leaving only the real.

Similarly, we can rationalize news coverage by pairing articles from authors with complementary emotional valences—balancing positive-leaning and negative-leaning perspectives on the same topic to approach more neutral, comprehensive understanding.
